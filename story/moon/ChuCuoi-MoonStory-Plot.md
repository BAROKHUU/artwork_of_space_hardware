# The Legend of Cuội on the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a woodcutter named Cuội. One day, while walking through the forest, he suddenly encountered a tiger cub charging towards him. With no time to escape, he bravely raised his axe and fought the tiger. Since the tiger cub was still young and weaker than a human, Cuội struck it with his axe, and it fell to the ground.

Just then, the mother tiger returned. Cuội quickly threw down his axe and climbed up a nearby tree. From above, he saw the mother tiger run to a bush nearby, pick some leaves, and chew them before feeding them to her cub. After a short while, the tiger cub began to move, wagged its tail, and miraculously came back to life.

After the mother tiger had carried her cub away, Cuội went to the bush, dug up the plant by its roots, and took it home.

With this precious medicinal plant, Cuội was able to save the lives of many people. One day, he saved the daughter of a wealthy man. In gratitude, the man gave his daughter's hand in marriage to Cuội. The couple lived happily together in a peaceful and loving home.

However, one day, Cuội's wife accidentally slipped and fell, seriously injuring her head. Cuội applied the medicinal leaves to her wound, but she did not wake up. Desperate to save his beloved wife, Cuội tried making a brain out of clay for her and then applied the medicinal leaves once again. To his surprise, his wife came back to life and looked as healthy as before. However, from that day on, she began to suffer from forgetfulness.

One day, Cuội's wife forgot her husband's warning and watered the magical plant with her urine. As soon as she finished, the precious plant slowly began to float up into the sky. Seeing this, Cuội rushed over and grabbed its roots. But the plant continued to rise higher and higher, pulling Cuội all the way up to the Moon.

To this day, whenever we look up at the Moon, we can still see Cuội sitting beneath the precious medicinal tree.

# PLOT STORY

Characters: Rover, Uncle Cuoi, Moon Rabbit, The Banyan tree

Location: Moon

Introduction:

On the Moon, where there is no wind to whisper and no rain to wash away the footprints, silence is absolute. But on one freezing night, right beneath the glowing branches of the ancient Banyan Tree, a rusty, forgotten wheel gave a sudden, metallic... CLICK.

For fifty years, the little Apollo Lunar Rover had stood completely still, buried under gray moondust. Everyone on Earth thought it was just a dead piece of space junk. But tonight, its dead screen flickered back to life with a single word blinking in the dark: TRANSMITTING...

#### 1. Awakening in the Dark

Wakened by the mysterious click, Uncle Cuoi and the Moon Rabbit rush over. They find the little Rover shivering under the cold starlight. Its battery is at 0%, and its metal joints are locked tight.

"My mission ended decades ago," the Rover clicks sadly, its voice full of static. "I'm just a pile of iron left behind. Nobody remembers me. Nobody is listening."

#### 2. Seeking the Wisdom of the Roots

Feeling its heavy heart, Uncle Cuoi and the Moon Rabbit guide the Rover to the ancient Banyan Tree - the cosmic guardian whose roots stretch deep into the moonrock, holding the memories of Earth and the stars together.

The Banyan Tree wraps a warm, glowing root gently around the Rover's chassis. "Little machine," the tree whispers, "immortality is not about moving forever. It is about what you leave behind."

The Banyan Tree begins to read the Rover's ancient hardware logs, explaining how its wire-mesh wheels and rock-sampling tools helped NASA scientists understand the Moon's secrets, paving the way for all future space exploration.

#### 3. The Plot Twist: The Delayed-Action Message

Just as the Rover starts to feel comforted, the Banyan Tree’s roots suddenly spark with bright, golden light. The tree gasps in surprise.

"Wait... your mission log isn't empty. There is a final outgoing transmission stuck in your queue from 1972!"

The Rover protests in confusion: "That's impossible! I ran out of power fifty years ago before I could send a final message!"

The Twist: The Banyan Tree reveals the truth: The Rover's final, accidental burst of data wasn't lost. Because there is no atmosphere on the Moon, that tiny pulse of radio energy bounced off a cosmic frequency and drifted through space for decades. It was a delayed-action time capsule.

#### 4. Find YOU in the Universe

The Banyan Tree projects the signal outward into a glowing leaf. The screen shows a live image of Earth, specifically, a child sitting at a desk late at night, staring wide-eyed at a drawing of a lunar rover.

As the child looks up through their bedroom window at the Moon, a realization hits. The signal the Rover sent half a century ago has just now reached Earth, landing directly in that child's imagination as a sudden spark of inspiration.

The child whispers to the night sky: "One day, I'm going to build machines that go up there."

#### 5. Resolution

The little Rover’s screen stops blinking. It is no longer sad. It realizes it wasn’t "abandoned" by accident, it was planted on the Moon like a cosmic seed, waiting for the exact right moment to wake up a new dreamer.

Under the glowing branches of the Banyan Tree, the Rover finally rests, knowing that its wheels may never turn again, but its dream is alive and well on Earth.

## The Roots of the Galaxy: The Iron Heart and the Immortal Banyan Tree

### Story Synopsis

Long ago, on the Moon's quiet surface, an old lunar rover rested beneath the branches of a magical Banyan Tree. Once part of an ambitious human mission, the little machine had helped astronauts explore the lunar landscape. But after the mission ended, it was left behind, far away from Earth.

As time passed, the Rover began to feel forgotten. It wondered whether its journey had meant anything at all. “What happens when my mission is over? Will anyone remember me?” it beeped sadly.

Noticing the Rover's fading spirit, Uncle Cuoi and the playful Moon Rabbit took it to meet the ancient Banyan Tree, the guardian of memories between Earth and the stars.

With gentle wisdom, the Banyan Tree explained that immortality does not mean living forever. It means leaving something meaningful behind. Just as a tree grows new branches from its roots, knowledge grows from every discovery. The Rover's journey, its observations, and the Moon rocks explored by astronauts had all become part of humanity's growing understanding of the Moon.

When the Banyan Tree touches the Rover, its glowing roots don't just read thoughts—they plug into the Rover's historical data ports. The roots read the titanium frame, the wire-mesh wheel tracks left in the lunar dust, and the last remaining telemetry logs. The tree explains: "Your metal wheels left tracks that will stay undisturbed on the Moon for millions of years because there is no wind or rain. Your engineering is a permanent chapter of Earth's history."

Inspired by the Banyan Tree, the Moon Rabbit suggested that the Rover did not need to remain active to be remembered. Its greatest power was the knowledge and inspiration it had left behind.

Then came a magical surprise. One of the Banyan Tree's glowing leaves revealed a little child on Earth, studying NASA's lunar exploration photographs and drawing a rover of their own. Looking up at the Moon, the child whispered, “One day, I want to build machines that help people explore the universe!”

At that moment, the Rover understood. Its mission had not truly ended. Although its wheels might never move again, its legacy was inspiring a new generation of explorers.

Under the glowing branches of the Banyan Tree, the Rover finally felt at peace. It was no longer just an old machine left on the Moon. It was a seed of knowledge, helping future dreams take root.

### Educational Message

The Banyan Tree connects roots to branches, just as science connects past discoveries to future possibilities. Space exploration is not only about reaching distant worlds; it is also about learning, sharing knowledge, and inspiring others.

# **FIND YOU IN THE UNIVERSE**

# *“Old roots hold tight; new dreams take flight”*

# **Characters**

* # Narrator

* # ROVER – A little scientific package from Apollo 11\.

* # Uncle Cuội – A friendly guardian of the Moon.

* # Moon Rabbit – A curious and playful companion.

* # Banyan Tree – A wise guardian of memories.

* # Child – A young dreamer on Earth.

—----------------------------

**SCENE 1\. OPENING**

*In The Moon. Dark. A tiny sound.*

NARRATOR: Every night, Uncle Cuội sits under his banyan tree. Everyone knows two things about him: he loves telling stories, and he appears every full moon.

*On the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival, the Moon shone brighter than ever.*

*Beneath his ancient Banyan Tree, Uncle Cuội was telling the Moon Rabbit an old story.*

*Suddenly, a tiny sound broke the silence."*

MOON RABBIT: Uncle\! What’s that sound?

UNCLE CUỘI: (whispering) Tick... tick... Rabbit, I think something is *alive* out there.

MOON RABBIT: Is this another one of your tall tales?

UNCLE CUỘI: Not this time. Come on\!

**—--------------------------------------**

**SCENE 2\. THE LITTLE ONE IN THE DUST**

Sea of Tranquillity. A small metal package sits half-buried in grey dust.

NARRATOR: In 1969, two astronauts walked here: [Neil Armstrong](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F05b6w&q=Neil+Armstrong) and [Buzz Aldrin](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0hfml&q=Buzz+Aldrin). A third, Michael, waited in orbit. Before they left, they set down a small package of instruments.

ROVER: Hello? Are you... real?

MOON RABBIT: (jumping back) It talks\!

ROVER: I’m ROVER. I had a job. I had an ear for listening to the Moon shake, and a little mirror. And then everyone left. Did they forget me?

UNCLE CUỘI: Why would they leave you here?

NARRATOR: The astronauts collected Moon rocks and deployed scientific instruments designed to remain on the lunar surface, sending their discoveries back to Earth." 

—-----------------------------------

**SCENE 3\. THE ROVER THAT WENT QUIET**

Under the banyan tree. The roots glow softly.

BANYAN TREE: Tell me, ROVER. What did your ear hear?

**ROVER:**

"I listened for moonquakes and space rocks hitting the Moon. I worked for about three weeks. Then, one day, my signals stopped."

**MOON RABBIT:**

"Only three weeks? Was that all?"

**BANYAN TREE:**

"Three weeks was enough to begin something extraordinary. Your discoveries helped scientists build better instruments for future missions."

(Pause. ROVER’s light flickers.)

ROVER: But now everything is silent. Nobody calls, nobody answers. I think I’ve been forgotten.

—---------------------------

**SCENE 4\. THE COLDEST NIGHT *(climax)***

The Moon’s long night. Stars are sharp. Cuội and Rabbit huddle by ROVER.

MOON RABBIT: (softly) ROVER... is the mirror still working?

ROVER: It’s just glass. It has no battery and no wires. It can’t call anyone. It can only wait.

UNCLE CUỘI: (quietly, for the first time without a joke) I know that feeling. I tell the truth and nobody believes me. Maybe we’re all just waiting.

Silence. ROVER’s light dims almost to nothing.

**—--------------------------------------------------------------**

**SCENE 5\. THE TWIST: A THIN LINE OF LIGHT**

A faint green beam streaks up from Earth and strikes ROVER’s mirror. It flashes back.

**MOON RABBIT:**

"Look\! A light from Earth\!"

**BANYAN TREE:**

"That's a laser\! Scientists are sending light toward your little mirror."

**ROVER:**

"My mirror? After all these years?"

**BANYAN TREE:**

"Yes\! It reflects the light to Earth, helping scientists measure the distance to the Moon."

**ROVER:**

"So... I'm still helping them discover something?"

**BANYAN TREE:**

"Of course. You were never forgotten, little one."

**ROVER:**

"Then my story isn't over\!"

—------------------------------

**SCENE 6\. FIND YOU**

**BANYAN TREE:** "Look, little one. Your discoveries have reached further than you can imagine."

***The golden leaf reveals a child in Vietnam drawing a picture of the Moon.***

**EASEP:** "Someone is dreaming about exploring the Moon?"

**UNCLE CUỘI:** "Yes. And perhaps one day, that child will discover something we have never imagined."

**MOON RABBIT:** "Maybe the next great explorer is right there\!"

*The child slowly turns toward the audience.*

**CHILD:** "Maybe that explorer could be me..."

**(Pause)**

"Or maybe... YOU\!"

**NARRATOR:** "Every great discovery begins with a curious mind. And every dream begins with someone who dares to imagine."

**ALL CHARACTERS:**

**"FIND YOU IN THE UNIVERSE\!"**

*—-------------------------*

**CLOSING SCENE** 

 Your roots remind you where you come from. Your dreams decide how far you can go.

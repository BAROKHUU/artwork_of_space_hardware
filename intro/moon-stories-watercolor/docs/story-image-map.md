## Implemented pacing

51 authored caption beats become 97 consecutive display units (maximum 38 characters each). Every unit retains its speaker. Image transitions span 0.65 timeline units, or 0.95 for images 11–13. Caption units span 1.12 normally, 1.35 for images 11–13, and 0.95 for images 16–20. One timeline unit corresponds to 68% of the stage height in scroll distance. Caption fade-in/out use the first/last 16% of the interval. The final line never fades out and receives a further 2.2-unit hold. All intervals depend on scroll position, not elapsed time.
